Truss Configuration

Seeds the Custom Truss Joints/Members editor below with the CURRENT truss's exact joints and members, then switches to Custom truss so you can add, remove, or drag any of them. Re-selecting a truss type above starts fresh again.

Drives the truss web-member pattern and panel joints only.

Independent of the truss panel count above — the top chord/rafter is automatically subdivided wherever a purlin lands, so adding/removing a purlin never adds/removes a truss panel, and vice versa.

Bottom chord is always flat at eave level. In Auto mode, ridge-depth simply equals the roof rise (classic triangular roof truss). Custom mode raises the whole top chord uniformly, giving a constant-depth parallel-chord truss.
